Inga in the browser

Beta. A first public test of Inga in the browser: pick a model, chat, run the Standard test, then press "Report on GitHub" at the bottom (what works and what does not: the beta's guide).

An experiment: Inga compiled to WebAssembly, running on this device's CPU (WebAssembly SIMD; one thread, or every core when the page is cross-origin isolated), or with its layers on the GPU through WebGPU (Settings, Device: experimental). A model larger than the tab's memory runs with part of it read from this site's storage at every token. Nothing is sent anywhere: the model runs in this page. Tested in headless Chromium on Linux and in Safari on an iPhone 16 Pro.
