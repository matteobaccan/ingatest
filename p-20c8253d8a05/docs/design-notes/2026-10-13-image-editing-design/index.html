Image editing: image to image and inpainting

Status: for 0.30.0 (the feature survey of 2026-10-13, rank 4: “Image editing”; TODO.md); built in the branch feat/image-edit (“As built” at the end). Flux Kontext and Flux Fill stay with image generation part 3. Code: crates/inga-diffusion/src/vae.rs (Encoder), edit.rs (Edit, Picture), pipeline.rs (GenParams::edit, the start, the blending, the inpainting UNet), files.rs (diffusers’ encoder names); src/commands/image.rs, src/cli.rs; crates/inga-server/src/images.rs (POST /v1/images/edits); webui.html (the picture editor); tools/vae_encoder_oracle.py.

1. Why

Section titled “1. Why”
  • Every image tool of the survey edits pictures. stable-diffusion.cpp (-i, --strength, --mask), KoboldCpp, InvokeAI, LocalAI and Lemonade take a picture and a mask; OpenAI’s API has POST /v1/images/edits, which clients (Open WebUI, AnythingLLM, LibreChat) call. Inga drew from text only: vae.rs had the decoder, not the encoder a picture needs to become latents.
  • The cheapest useful edits need no new model. Image to image and inpainting by latent blending run on the checkpoints people already have (SD 1.x / 2.x, SDXL, and the flow models SD 3.x and Flux.1); the inpainting checkpoints (a UNet of 9 input channels: sd-v1-5-inpainting, stable-diffusion-2-inpainting, sd_xl_base_1.0_inpainting) do better at the seams and cost one more input.

2. What

Section titled “2. What”

2.1 The VAE encoder

Section titled “2.1 The VAE encoder”
  • vae::Encoder, read from first_stage_model.encoder.* (and quant_conv) as diffusers’ AutoencoderKL computes it: conv_in; per level the ResNet blocks, then a downsampler but at the last level (a 3 x 3 convolution of stride 2 on the map padded by one row and one column of zeros after it: F.pad(x, (0, 1, 0, 1)), not the convolution’s symmetric padding); the middle block (ResNet, single-head attention, ResNet); GroupNorm and SiLU; conv_out to twice the latent channels; quant_conv when the file has it (Flux’s and SD 3’s VAEs have none). The latents are the mean of the distribution (latent_dist.mean), not a sample: the same picture gives the same latents, and the VAEs’ variance is tiny.
  • The denoiser reads (mean - shift) * scale, the inverse of what the decoder undoes (Decoder::latents): 0.18215 for 1.x / 2.x, 0.13025 for SDXL, SD 3’s and Flux’s scale and shift.
  • Where it comes from: a whole checkpoint, a diffusers folder (vae/’s encoder.down_blocks.* renamed to the original names) or --vae FILE. A VAE without its encoder still draws from text; an edit then says what to give (--vae with a whole VAE). ImageModel::edits() tells, /v1/models says it ("edits").

2.2 Image to image

Section titled “2.2 Image to image”
  • GenParams::edit = Some(Edit { image, mask: None, strength }): the picture, resized to the image’s size (Lanczos), is encoded; the schedule’s sigmas (the discrete schedule’s, or the flow models’ shifted ones) are cut to their last int(steps * strength) steps (diffusers’ get_timesteps; a rounding error of the product forgiven); the start is the picture’s latents noised to the first sigma left (x + sigma n for the DDPM models, (1 - sigma) x + sigma n for a flow), the noise the seed’s as for text to image. A strength that runs no step is refused with the numbers (a strength of 0.1 runs none of the 4 steps).
  • Default strength 0.75 (stable-diffusion.cpp’s and the AUTOMATIC1111 web UI’s); 1 with a mask (diffusers’ inpainting pipeline).
  • The size: given, or the picture’s proportions with its longer side at the model’s default side (512; 1024 for SDXL, SD 3.x, Flux), each side rounded to the model’s multiple (edit_size): a 4000 x 3000 photo is drawn at 512 x 384.

2.3 Inpainting

Section titled “2.3 Inpainting”
  • The mask is a picture of any size, resized to the image’s (bilinear): white is repainted, black kept, grey mixed; a PNG with transparent pixels is read by its transparency instead (transparent is repainted: OpenAI’s masks). Without a mask, a picture with transparent pixels is its own mask (OpenAI’s rule).
  • Ordinary checkpoints: latent blending (ComfyUI’s KSamplerX0Inpaint). The mask is brought to the latents (a latent pixel is repainted when any of its 8 x 8 pixels is at least half masked: a thin stroke is not lost); at every step the denoiser’s input outside the mask is the picture noised to the current sigma, and its output outside the mask is the picture’s latents, so the last step lands on them. Both samplers (Euler, Euler ancestral) and the flow models take it unchanged.
  • Inpainting checkpoints (a UNet of 9 input channels, now loaded instead of refused): the UNet’s input is the latents, then the mask at latent size, then the latents of the masked picture (the masked pixels set to 0, grey, as diffusers’ prepare_mask_and_masked_image). No blending. Without a mask the whole picture is repainted; without a picture (text to image) the mask is all ones and the picture grey, as stable-diffusion.cpp does. The encoder is then required at load.
  • The pixels outside the mask are the picture’s: after decoding, the image is mask * generated + (1 - mask) * picture at full resolution (the autoencoder’s round trip blurs what was not to change).

2.4 The web UI

Section titled “2.4 The web UI”
  • Image mode’s composer has a Picture button (image-only; the chat’s attach buttons stay hidden there). A PNG or JPEG chosen opens the editor above the message box: the picture on a canvas at its own size, a second canvas over it where the brush paints the mask (semi-transparent red; the brush’s size, an eraser, Clear mask), the strength (empty: the server’s default) and Remove. Pointer events, so a finger or a pen paints on a phone or a tablet.
  • Send posts OpenAI’s form to /v1/images/edits: the picture, the mask as a grey PNG at the picture’s size (white where painted; none when nothing is painted: image to image), the prompt, the strength and the settings panel’s size, steps, guidance, seed and negative prompt. The picture and its mask stay with the user’s message, so Regenerate and Edit (of the message) redo the same edit; the answer’s stats say 512×512, 15 of 20 steps (strength 0.75), cfg 7, seed 42, 30.2 s.
  • A generated picture has an Edit button: it goes into the editor, to be edited again.
  • A model whose VAE has no encoder ("edits": false in /v1/models) disables the button, whose tooltip says why and what to give.
  • Fifteen new texts, in the ten languages.

2.5 The command line and the API

Section titled “2.5 The command line and the API”
  • inga image MODEL -p TEXT --init-image FILE [--strength X] [--mask FILE]; the terminal says what it does: editing in.png (1024x768 to 512x384), strength 0.75: 15 of 20 steps, inpainting: the picture blended back outside the mask at each step. --strength and --mask without --init-image, or a strength outside 0..1, are refused.
  • POST /v1/images/edits (multipart, up to 32 MiB as the other uploads): OpenAI’s image (or image[], the first), mask, prompt, n, size, response_format (b64_json only, as the generations), with strength and the generations’ extensions (negative_prompt, steps, cfg_scale, guidance, seed, sampler). The answer’s inga object adds strength, steps_run, mask and inpainting_unet. Errors are 400 with the reason (no image, a file that is not a picture, a size the model cannot take, no encoder).

3. How it is checked

Section titled “3. How it is checked”
  • The encoder against diffusers: tools/vae_encoder_oracle.py encodes a 64 x 64 test picture with the tiny 1.x checkpoint’s VAE through diffusers.AutoencoderKL when torch and diffusers are installed, else through a float64 NumPy transcription of the same modules; crates/inga-diffusion/tests/edit.rs compares eight values, the mean and the mean absolute value (within 1e-4 and 1e-5). The numbers in the test came from the NumPy path: PyTorch’s wheels could not be installed on the machine that wrote them (download.pytorch.org refused; PyPI’s CUDA build too large for its disk). Run the script with diffusers once to confirm (TODO.md, real-world checks).
  • Image to image: the steps run for strengths 1, 0.75, 0.5 and 0.3 of 4 steps (4, 3, 2, 1), the progress (Text, Encode, the steps), a lower strength nearer the picture’s latents, the refusals (no step, strength 1.5, a mask of the wrong size, a picture of another size).
  • Inpainting by blending: with Euler and Euler ancestral, the latents outside the mask are the picture’s (within 1e-5), those inside differ; the pixels outside the mask are the picture’s exactly. On Flux (flow) too.
  • The inpainting UNet: a tiny 9-channel checkpoint (testing::Config::unet_in) draws with a mask, without one, and from text; without the encoder it is refused with --vae.
  • Diffusers folders carry the encoder (the same latents as the whole checkpoint).
  • The CLI and the server (tests/images.rs): inga image --init-image --mask keeps the unmasked pixels, sizes from the picture’s proportions, refuses a file that is not a picture; POST /v1/images/edits gives the CLI’s image for the same settings, reads a transparent mask, and answers 400 for six bad forms.
  • The web UI (crates/inga-server/tests/webui_image_edit.{rs,cjs}, headless Chromium): the button in image mode only, the editor at the picture’s size, a brush stroke sent as a white mask at the picture’s size and black elsewhere with the form’s fields, the editor closed and the stats, Edit on a generated picture, no mask when nothing is painted, the button disabled without an encoder.

4. Not in it

Section titled “4. Not in it”
  • Flux Kontext (an instruction edit: the picture’s tokens beside the latents) and Flux Fill (Flux’s inpainting transformer, 384 input channels) come with image generation part 3 (Qwen-Image, Flux 2, the SDXL refiner).
  • SD 3.x inpainting checkpoints and instruct-pix2pix (8 input channels, refused with the reason).
  • A mask’s blur or growth, “only masked” cropping (A1111’s “inpaint at full resolution”), outpainting (a canvas larger than the picture), a sampled latent (latent_dist.sample()) instead of the mean.
  • Live previews of an edit (the “Live previews” item, TAESD).

As built

Section titled “As built”

As §2. Choices made while building it:

  • The encoder returns the mean only; ImageModel::encode applies the family’s scale and shift, so the tests and the inpainting UNet’s masked picture share it.
  • The latent mask takes the largest of each 8 x 8 block (thresholded at 0.5) rather than diffusers’ nearest pixel, both for the blending and for the inpainting UNet’s mask channel; the pixel overlay keeps the mask’s soft edges.
  • The picture’s and the mask’s PNG alpha is read by a second decode (inga_vision::decode_alpha), not by changing Image, which every vision model uses as RGB.
  • inga-diffusion now depends on inga-vision (decoding and Pillow’s resizing).
  • Flux and SD 3.x edit too (image to image and blending): the same start and blending in the flow’s terms, checked on the tiny Flux model.
