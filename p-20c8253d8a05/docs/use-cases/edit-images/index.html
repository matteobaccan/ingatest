Edit a picture: transform, touch up, extend, upscale

What you get

Section titled “What you get”

A picture of yours redrawn from a prompt, on the CPU: all of it (image to image: a photo turned into a watercolour, a sketch into a render) or only the part you paint over (inpainting: a sky replaced, an object removed), the rest of the picture kept pixel for pixel. inga image --init-image writes a PNG file; inga serve offers the same to the browser (the image mode’s picture editor, with a brush) and to OpenAI clients (POST /v1/images/edits). It works with ordinary Stable Diffusion 1.x / 2.x and SDXL checkpoints, with their inpainting checkpoints, and with SD 3.x and Flux.1. A picture can also be extended past its borders (outpainting: the canvas grown, the new part drawn) and upscaled 2 or 4 times (by Real-ESRGAN, or a resize, then its details redrawn by tiles); the web UI calls image to image Transform.

Requirements

Section titled “Requirements”
  • What generate-images.md needs: Windows, Linux or macOS, the model’s file size plus 2 to 8 GB of free RAM.
  • A model whose VAE has its encoder: a whole checkpoint (what the AUTOMATIC1111 web UI and ComfyUI load), a diffusers folder, or --vae with a whole VAE file. A VAE cut to its decoder draws from text but cannot edit (Inga says so).
  • A picture, PNG or JPEG; for inpainting, a mask (a picture of the same proportions: white where to repaint, black elsewhere), or the web UI’s brush.

1. Get Inga

Section titled “1. Get Inga”

Download the archive for your system from the Releases page and put inga on your PATH (details); inga --version checks it.

2. Get a model

Section titled “2. Get a model”

Any checkpoint of generate-images.md edits. For inpainting, an inpainting checkpoint (a UNet of 9 input channels) repaints with cleaner seams: Stable Diffusion 1.5’s (stable-diffusion-v1-5/stable-diffusion-inpainting on Hugging Face), stabilityai/stable-diffusion-2-inpainting or SDXL’s (diffusers/stable-diffusion-xl-1.0-inpainting-0.1), as a .safetensors file or a diffusers folder (.ckpt pickles are not read). SD-Turbo (hf://stabilityai/sd-turbo/sd_turbo.safetensors) edits fastest: 2 to 4 steps at strength 0.5 to 1.

3. Redraw a picture

Section titled “3. Redraw a picture”

The strength says how much is redrawn: the sampler skips the first part of its schedule and starts from the picture’s latents noised to the step the strength implies (0.6 of 4 steps: the last 2). Near 0 the picture barely changes; at 1 only its colours and layout survive. Without --strength, 0.75. Without --width / --height, the picture keeps its proportions with its longer side at the model’s size (512; 1024 for SDXL, SD 3.x and Flux).

4. Repaint a part (inpainting)

Section titled “4. Repaint a part (inpainting)”

Paint the mask in any editor (white where to repaint, black where to keep; grey mixes), or take one with transparent pixels (OpenAI’s masks: transparent is repainted):

With an ordinary checkpoint the line says inpainting: the picture blended back outside the mask at each step: the sampler is given the picture again outside the mask at every step, so only the masked part is drawn. Either way the pixels outside the mask are the picture’s own, untouched. With a mask the strength defaults to 1 (the masked part redrawn from noise); a lower one keeps more of what was there.

5. Extend a picture (outpainting)

Section titled “5. Extend a picture (outpainting)”

--extend grows the picture’s canvas: 16:9 (an aspect ratio: the smallest canvas of it that holds the picture, the picture in the middle), 1024x768 (a size), 128 (128 pixels on every side) or left=256,right=256 (also top=, bottom=, x=, y=). The new part starts as the picture’s edge repeated (--extend-fill noise for noise around its mean colour) and is masked, with a band of 16 pixels of the picture along each new border so the seam is redrawn; then it is an inpainting as in step 4, at strength 1 by default. An inpainting checkpoint reads the mask itself and draws the best borders; an ordinary checkpoint gets the picture blended back outside the mask at every step, and a seam may show. The line before the drawing says which:

A model whose VAE has no encoder in Inga (Qwen-Image) cannot extend; the web UI’s Extend says so for the loaded model. Unverified against another outpainting tool on real checkpoints.

6. Upscale a picture

Section titled “6. Upscale a picture”

Real-ESRGAN x4plus (Comfy-Org/Real-ESRGAN, the BSD-3-Clause weights of xinntao/Real-ESRGAN as safetensors, 66,857,836 bytes) makes the picture 4 times larger on the CPU, by tiles of 192 pixels blended across 16, so a large picture fits in memory; asked for --upscale 2 it is followed by a Lanczos resize down. Any RRDBNet file in BasicSR’s names (x2plus, the anime 6B) or the older ESRGAN form loads too (--upscaler FILE). Measured: a 64 x 64 picture to 256 x 256 in 2.2 s on 4 threads of this project’s build container (the test build, opt-level = 1); larger pictures and other machines unmeasured.

With a drawing model as well, --detail S redraws the fine details after the upscale: image to image at strength S by tiles of the model’s side, overlapping by 64 pixels:

Without --upscaler the picture is enlarged by a Lanczos resize, and the detail pass then runs at 0.3 unless --detail says otherwise; the terminal says what was done.

7. Edit from the browser or a client

Section titled “7. Edit from the browser or a client”

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080/. In image mode the Picture button (above the message box) opens a picture in the editor: paint with the brush where to repaint (its size next to it, an Eraser, Clear mask), set the Strength if you like, type what to draw and press Enter. With nothing painted the whole picture is redrawn. Each generated picture has an Edit button that puts it back into the editor, to go on editing; Regenerate redoes the same edit with a new seed. The menu’s Images page has the modes as tabs: Transform (image to image), Inpaint, Extend (the shape to grow to or the pixels on each side, and how the loaded model will draw it) and Upscale (2 or 4 times, Real-ESRGAN or a resize, the detail strength; with no detail pass it needs no image model).

Clients send OpenAI’s form:

Besides OpenAI’s image (or image[]), mask, prompt, n, size and response_format (b64_json), the server reads strength and the generations’ negative_prompt, steps, cfg_scale, guidance, seed and sampler; the answer’s inga object says the strength, the steps run and whether a mask and an inpainting UNet were used. extend (and extend_fill) on the same route extends the picture; POST /v1/images/upscale takes image, scale (2 or 4), upscaler (a path, hf:// file or kit://realesrgan-x4plus; none: a resize) and detail, with the prompt and the settings of the detail pass (docs/api/inga.yaml).

Options worth knowing

Section titled “Options worth knowing”
Option Meaning
--init-image FILE The picture to edit (PNG or JPEG)
--strength X How much is redrawn, 0 to 1: the share of --steps run (0.75; 1 with a mask)
--mask FILE Where to repaint: white (or transparent) repainted, black kept, any size
--extend SPEC Grow the canvas: A:B, WxH, N, left=N,top=N,right=N,bottom=N
--extend-fill F What the new part starts as: edge (default) or noise
--upscale N Make the picture 2 or 4 times larger
--upscaler FILE A Real-ESRGAN file (else a Lanczos resize)
--detail S The detail pass’s strength after the upscale (0 with --upscaler, 0.3 without)
--width N, --height N The image’s size (default: the picture’s proportions at the model’s size)
--steps N, --cfg X, --seed N, --sampler S As for text to image

How it works

Section titled “How it works”
  • The picture is resized to the image’s size (Lanczos) and turned into latents by the VAE’s encoder (diffusers’ AutoencoderKL.encode, the mean of its distribution), checked against diffusers’ modules on a tiny checkpoint (tools/vae_encoder_oracle.py).
  • The schedule is cut to its last steps * strength steps; the picture’s latents, noised by the seed’s noise to the first sigma left, are where the sampler starts.
  • An ordinary checkpoint inpaints by blending: the denoiser’s input outside the mask is the picture noised to the current sigma, its output there the picture’s latents (ComfyUI’s way). An inpainting checkpoint’s UNet reads 9 channels: the latents, the mask and the latents of the picture with the masked part grey.
  • After decoding, the pixels outside the mask are put back from the picture.

Troubleshooting

Section titled “Troubleshooting”
  • this model's VAE has no encoder: the file’s VAE was cut to its decoder (some files and --vae downloads are). Give a whole VAE with --vae FILE, for example the checkpoint’s original vae/diffusion_pytorch_model.safetensors.
  • a strength of 0.1 runs none of the 4 steps: raise the strength or the steps (steps * strength must reach 1).
  • an inpainting UNet without the VAE's encoder: an inpainting checkpoint always needs the encoder, even from text; see the first point.
  • a UNet of 8 input channels (an instruct-pix2pix checkpoint?): instruct-pix2pix is not read; Flux Kontext and Flux Fill come with image generation part 3.
  • The seam shows around the repainted part: paint the mask a little larger than the object, use an inpainting checkpoint, or lower the strength.
